Which expense categories require an asset?

When a user records a voucher line in a ticked category, they must pick the asset the cost is for. If they hold no asset of the required type, the field is skipped (a manager reviews the unlinked line at approval time). Fuel is always unticked — it's a general operating cost, not an asset cost.
